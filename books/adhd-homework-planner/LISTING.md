## Description
Homework, one page at a time.

This undated planner is designed for ADHD students and anyone who struggles with getting started, keeping track of steps, losing time, or making careless mistakes at the finish line. Each box on the daily page is there for one real executive-function problem:

- **Recharge First**: snack, water, move and quiet time before homework starts
- **My Top 3 + a tiny 2-minute first step**: makes starting feel small
- **Homework table with every step written down**: the page remembers so your brain doesn't have to
- **Guess vs Real minutes**: builds a feel for how long things take
- **Focus Sprints** with movement breaks, plus a **hyperfocus alarm**
- **Distraction Parking Lot**: write the random thought down and get back to work
- **Before I Hand It In** checklist to catch careless mistakes
- **Win of the Day + My Reward**: notice the progress

Inside: a full school year — 180 undated daily pages, 36 weekly "My Week" pages (due dates, after-school schedule, homework streak, friendship check-in with ready-to-use phrases), 9 monthly reset pages, a tests & projects tracker, a "How My Brain Works Best" card to share with teachers, and a Wins Wall.

Undated, so you can start any day, in any school year (2026-2027, 2027-2028 and beyond). Large 8.5 x 11 pages with plenty of room to write.

*This is a planning tool, not a medical or therapeutic product.*
