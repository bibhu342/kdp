## Description
Tons of dino fun in one book! Dinosaurs who drive diggers, race karts, fire trucks and cranes star in 42 activities for kids ages 4-8.

- 12 dino truck coloring pages
- 12 mazes, from easy to tricky
- 12 word searches: Digger Day, Speedy Racers, Rescue Squad, Big Machines, Dino World and more

- Answers at the back, plus an Activity Champion certificate
- Single-sided activity pages, so markers won't bleed through
- Large 8.5 x 11 inch pages

Great for road trips, rainy days and quiet time, with no screens needed.
