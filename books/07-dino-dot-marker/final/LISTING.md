## Description
Big dots for little hands!

A first dot marker book for toddlers who love dinosaurs and trucks. Kids press a dot into each big circle to fill in shapes, numbers and letters, a fun way to build hand control, colors and early learning.

- 8 shapes: circle, square, triangle, heart, star, diamond, oval, rectangle
- Numbers 1-10, each with a counting row
- The alphabet A to Z with a simple word for every letter
- Extra-big circles sized for standard dot markers and bingo daubers
- Single-sided pages, so markers won't bleed onto the next activity
- A dino truck picture to color on every page, plus a certificate at the end
- Large 8.5 x 11 inch pages

Works with dot markers, stickers, crayons or finger paint.
