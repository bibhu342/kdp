## Description
Ready, set, trace! A first pen control workbook for preschoolers who love dinosaurs and trucks.

Kids trace dotted roads from a start dot to a friendly dino truck, building the pencil control they need before writing letters.

- Level 1: straight lines
- Level 2: waves
- Level 3: zigzags
- Level 4: bumps and steps
- Level 5: loops
- Level 6: mixed paths
- Top-to-bottom tracing pages, 6 shape pages (circle, square, triangle, heart, star, diamond) and 4 spirals
- Lines go left to right and top to bottom, just like real writing
- Tips for grown-ups and a Pencil Pro certificate
- Large 8.5 x 11 inch pages
