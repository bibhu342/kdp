## Description
Snip, snip, dino! A first scissor skills book for preschoolers who love dinosaurs and trucks.

Kids practise cutting along dashed lines that get a little harder at every level. Each page has friendly dino truck pictures to color before cutting.

- Level 1: straight lines
- Level 2: zigzags
- Level 3: wavy lines
- Level 4: tricky paths (steps, spikes and curves)
- Level 5: shapes (circle, square, triangle, heart, star, diamond, oval, hexagon)
- Level 6: dino truck cut-outs to color, cut and play with
- Single-sided pages, so cutting one page never ruins the next
- Scissor safety tips, practice snips and a Super Scissor Star certificate
- Large 8.5 x 11 inch pages

Builds hand strength, coordination and focus, getting little hands ready for school.
