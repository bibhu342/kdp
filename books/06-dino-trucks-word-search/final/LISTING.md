## Description
Can you find the dino? 60 fun word search puzzles for kids who love dinosaurs and trucks!

Every puzzle has a theme kids know: dinosaurs, diggers, monster trucks, fire rescue, space, the ocean, farm animals, sports and more. Plus a dino truck picture to color on every page.

A screen-free activity for road trips, quiet time and rainy days.
